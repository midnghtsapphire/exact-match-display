# Brand Guidelines

## Brand name

- **Primary:** SubKill
- **Repository / internal:** Exact Match Display

## Voice and tone

- Direct, clear, and outcomes-focused
- Confident but not alarmist
- Uses practical language around savings, control, and clarity

## Visual direction

- Dark-mode-first with high-contrast accent colors
- Bold display typography for conversion sections
- Clean dashboard cards emphasizing key metrics (spending, renewals, savings)

## Messaging pillars

1. **Clarity:** know every recurring charge.
2. **Control:** decide what to keep and what to cancel.
3. **Savings:** turn visibility into monthly savings.
