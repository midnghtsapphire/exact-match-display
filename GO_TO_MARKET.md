# Go-To-Market

## Product summary

Exact Match Display (SubKill) helps users identify and reduce recurring subscription spend through clear visibility, renewal reminders, and cancellation tracking.

## Target segments

- Consumers with 6+ monthly subscriptions
- Freelancers and creators managing mixed personal/business tools
- Small teams that want lightweight expense control without enterprise finance tooling

## Problem statement

Users lose money to low-visibility recurring charges. Existing finance tools often emphasize accounting over action-oriented cancellation insights.

## Positioning

- **Primary claim:** "Know exactly what subscriptions cost you every month, and cut waste fast."
- **Differentiator:** combined subscription tracking, cancellation metrics, and renewal visibility in a single focused UI.

## Launch channels

- Product Hunt launch with spend-optimization narrative
- Organic search pages targeting "cancel subscriptions", "reduce monthly app costs", "track recurring charges"
- Creator partnerships in budgeting and personal-finance niches

## Revenue model

- Free plan for basic tracking
- Paid plan for advanced analytics, reminders, and multi-account views

## Success metrics

- Activation: % of users adding at least 3 subscriptions within first session
- Retention: D30 active tracker users
- Monetization: free-to-paid conversion rate and average revenue per paid user
